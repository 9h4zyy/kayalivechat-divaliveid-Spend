Registration Guide

How to Become a Host and Join an Agency

1
Download Kaya Chat. Find it on Google Play or the App Store, or use an official referral link.
2
Create your account. Sign up with Google or your phone number. Google sign-up is recommended.
3
Complete your profile. Fill in the required information.
4
Open your profile. Tap Become a Host.
5
Verify your identity. Select Real Person Verification and follow the instructions.
6
Join an agency via “Profile” > “Become a Host” > “Join Agency” enter the invitation code provided to you (CVK6M5), and tap Confirm.

Kaya Chat Face Verification Tutorial

Click to expand
1
Open Kaya Chat and go to your Profile or Me page.
2
Tap Become a Host.
3
Select Real Person Verification.
4
Upload your own photo.
5
Tap Start Verification.
6
Follow the on-screen instructions. Keep your face clearly visible without a mask, sunglasses, or camera filters.
7
Once verification succeeds, tap Next to finish.
