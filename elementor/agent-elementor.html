Leadership Overview

What is Kaya Chat Agent?

A Kaya Chat Agent helps recruit, organize, and support hosts on the platform. Your role is to guide host activity, encourage quality content, and help your team follow Kaya Chat's rules.

Step-by-Step Guide

Kaya Chat Agent Registration Guide

1
Download Kaya Chat from Google Play or the App Store, or open the referral link right here.
2
Sign up using a Google account or your phone number. Google sign-up is recommended .
3
Fill in the required profile information.
4
Open your Profile page and copy your Kaya ID.
5
Open the official agent application link, then tap “Get” to request a verification code.
6
Return to Kaya Chat and open the message menu.
7
Tap “System Notification” and copy the verification code.
8
Return to the application page, paste the code, and tap “Submit.”
9
Open your Profile page and check “Agency Management” to view your agency information.

Commission Structure

Agency Commission Ratio

Your agency commission rate is linked to the valid diamond income generated by your team over the previous 30 days, excluding the current day.

Agency level Team gift income over the last 30 days Host income commission ratio
D 0–10M 12%
C 10M–50M 16%
B 50M–150M 18%
A 150M–300M 20%
S More than 300M 22%
Notes: The commission calculation formula is:
Agent commission = Host earnings × Commission rate

Commission Ratio Examples

1. Commission Rate Calculation

  • • A's effective total income = A + B + C + D + E + F + G + H + I + J + K = 395M (22% commission rate).
  • • B's effective total income = B + D + F + H + J = 148M (18% commission rate, as stated in the source).
  • • C's effective total income = C + E + G + I + K = 167M (20% commission rate).

2. Sub-agent commission formula:

• Sub-agent commission = (Master agent commission rate − Sub-agent commission rate) × total income of (T2 + T3 + T4 + T5 + T6).

3. Agent Commission from Sub-agents

  • • Commission A receives from B = (22% − 18%) × 148M = 5.92M.
  • • Commission A receives from C = (22% − 20%) × 167M = 3.34M.
  • • A's total commission from sub-agents = 5.92M + 3.34M = 9.26M, shown in the source as 926 USD.
Platform Standards

Agency Requirements

To work as an agency on Kaya Chat, you must follow the platform’s requirements

1

Minimum team: At least five active hosts.

2

Active host definition: Two hours on the mic per day, four days per week.

3

Host supervision: Guide hosts in creating quality content and ensure they follow platform rules.

4

Recruitment rules: Recruiting hosts inside the app and promoting other apps are prohibited.

5

Accountability: Agents may face penalties if their hosts violate platform rules.
