What is Kaya Chat?

Kaya Chat is a live voice party app where people come together to talk, meet new friends, and share moments in voice rooms.

Join conversations, interact with others, send gifts, earn coins, and climb the leaderboard. Want an active role? You can host your own room or explore becoming an agent who helps build and support a team of hosts.

Questions About Kaya Chat, Hosts, and Agents

Kaya Chat is a live voice party app where you can join voice rooms, meet new people, send virtual gifts, earn coins, and climb leaderboards. You can also host your own room or explore opportunities to build and support a host team.

A host focuses on voice-room activities, interacting with participants, and completing host tasks. An agent focuses on building and supporting a team of hosts, inviting hosts and sub-agents, and managing team activity. Each role has its own earning options and requirements.
